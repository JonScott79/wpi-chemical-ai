About WPI Predict

Advancing molecular property prediction through machine learning.

WPI Predict is an academic research platform developed at Worcester Polytechnic Institute to support molecular property prediction through machine learning. The platform provides researchers with a centralized, web-based environment for developing, evaluating, and deploying predictive models using SMILES chemical representations.

Our Mission

WPI Predict exists to accelerate computational chemistry research by providing an extensible platform for molecular property prediction. By combining modern machine learning, scientific software, and an intuitive user interface, the platform enables researchers to evaluate predictive models while providing a scalable foundation for future research.

How It Works

Researchers submit molecular structures using SMILES notation, select an available prediction model, and receive estimated molecular properties generated through machine learning. As new models are developed, they can be integrated into the platform, allowing WPI Predict to continually expand its research capabilities.

Technology

  • Machine Learning Models
  • SMILES Molecular Representations
  • Python Backend
  • Modern Web Interface
  • Extensible Model Architecture

Platform Goals

  • Support ongoing academic research.
  • Simplify molecular property prediction.
  • Provide a scalable platform for future models.
  • Encourage collaboration across research teams.
Research Platform

The WPI Predict Research Repository will continue to grow as new publications, datasets, prediction models, supplementary materials, and technical documentation become available through ongoing research at Worcester Polytechnic Institute.
